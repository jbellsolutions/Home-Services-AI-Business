# Ideal Customer Profile — Home Services AI Business

## Primary ICP: The Overwhelmed HVAC Owner

### Demographics
- **Title:** Owner, President, GM of an HVAC company
- **Revenue:** $1M-$5M annually
- **Employees:** 5-25 (including technicians)
- **Location:** US metros and suburbs (population 100K-2M is sweet spot)
- **Age:** 35-60
- **Tech comfort:** Uses a smartphone, can text, uses Facebook. NOT technical.
- **Years in business:** 5-20 years

### Psychographics
- Started as a technician, now runs the business
- Works 60-70 hour weeks
- Knows they're leaving money on the table but can't fix it
- Frustrated by hiring — "can't find good help"
- Suspicious of marketing agencies (been burned before)
- Trusts other contractors' recommendations
- Makes decisions based on ROI, not features
- Values: hard work, results, straight talk, no BS

### Current Tech Stack (What They Already Use)
- **Field Service:** ServiceTitan (25%), Housecall Pro (30%), Jobber (20%), paper/spreadsheets (25%)
- **Marketing:** Google Ads ($2-$10K/mo), Google LSA ($1-$5K/mo), maybe SEO agency
- **Phone:** Desk phone + personal cell. Maybe RingCentral or Grasshopper
- **Reviews:** Maybe asking techs to mention it. No system.
- **CRM:** Whatever their field service software has. Often unused.
- **Social media:** Facebook page with sporadic posts. No LinkedIn.

### Buying Triggers (When They're Ready)
1. Just lost a big job because they didn't answer the phone
2. Slow season — calendar has gaps, anxiety is high
3. Competitor is growing faster and they don't know why
4. Office manager quit — suddenly wearing all hats again
5. Marketing agency isn't delivering — "I'm paying $5K/mo for what?"
6. Just saw their 1-star Google review count climbing
7. Busy season is coming and they know they'll miss calls

### Where to Find Them
- **Google Maps:** Search "[trade] [city]" — every result is a potential lead
- **State contractor license databases** — Public records with names, addresses, license types
- **Google Business Profile:** Active profiles = active businesses
- **Yelp business listings** — Includes owner names, review counts, response rates
- **Facebook groups:** "HVAC Business Owners," "Plumbing Contractors Network," "Home Service Millionaire"
- **LinkedIn:** Titles like "Owner at [Company] HVAC," "President at [Company] Plumbing"
- **Trade associations:** ACCA, PHCC, NECA, NRCA member directories
- **Industry events:** AHR Expo, PHCC Connect, NATE conference, Service World Expo
- **Distributor relationships:** Carrier/Trane/Lennox dealer lists
- **BBB listings** — Accredited businesses with contact info
- **Angi/HomeAdvisor pro profiles** — Active contractors

## Secondary ICP: The Growth-Stage Multi-Trade Operator

### Demographics
- **Revenue:** $3M-$15M
- **Employees:** 25-100
- **Trades:** 2-4 (HVAC + plumbing, or HVAC + electrical + plumbing)
- **Structure:** Has an office manager or dispatcher. Maybe a marketing person.
- **Location:** Regional (2-5 service areas)

### Why They Buy
- Already spending $10K-$25K/mo on marketing
- Have the leads but can't process them fast enough
- Dispatch is a bottleneck
- Owner wants to step back from day-to-day
- Need systems to scale without adding headcount at the same rate

### What They Buy
- Tier 2 (Growth Machine) or Tier 3 (AI Ops Hub)
- Higher setup fees acceptable ($5K-$10K)
- Value the operations dashboard and dispatch AI
- Want white-glove service

## Tertiary ICP: The Home Services Franchise

### Demographics
- **Revenue:** $10M-$100M (across locations)
- **Locations:** 5-50
- **Trades:** Single trade usually (One Hour Heating & Air, Roto-Rooter, Mr. Electric)
- **Decision maker:** Franchise owner or regional VP

### Why They Buy
- Consistency across locations — same customer experience everywhere
- Centralizable — one system deployed across all locations
- Volume discount math works beautifully for them
- Corporate mandate to improve response times and reviews

### What They Buy
- Custom enterprise deployment
- $15K-$50K setup + $500-$1,000/location/mo
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom reporting for multi-location oversight

## Negative ICP: Who NOT to Target

| Don't Target | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Solo operators (<$300K revenue) | Can't afford $497/mo. Too small to benefit. |
| Handyman services | Low job value ($100-$200). ROI doesn't work. |
| New businesses (<2 years old) | No established lead flow to optimize. Need leads first, not lead management. |
| Companies with no online presence | If they have no Google Business Profile, they're not ready for this. |
| "I do everything myself" mindset | Won't adopt AI. Will fight the system. Will churn in 30 days. |
| Companies in rural areas (<50K population) | Limited market size. Hard to fill calendar even with perfect follow-up. |

## Outreach Personalization Data Points

When reaching out to a home services owner, research these for personalization:

1. **Google review count + average rating** — "I noticed you have 247 reviews at 4.7 stars..."
2. **Recent reviews (positive or negative)** — "I saw someone mentioned [specific thing]..."
3. **GBP response rate** — "I noticed 60% of your reviews don't have responses..."
4. **Service area** — "Serving the [city] metro area..."
5. **Specialties listed** — "I see you specialize in [specific service]..."
6. **Website quality** — "Your website mentions [service] — do you get a lot of calls for that?"
7. **After-hours availability** — "I called your number at 7 PM and got voicemail..."
8. **Google Ads activity** — "I see you're running Google Ads for [keyword]..."
9. **Technician count** — "With [X] technicians, you're probably running [X] calls a day..."
10. **Years in business** — "Congrats on [X] years in business..."

**The more specific the outreach, the higher the reply rate. Generic "home services" emails fail. "I noticed your HVAC company in Tampa has 247 Google reviews but only responds to 30% of them" converts.**
