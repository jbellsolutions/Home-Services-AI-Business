# Cold Email Sequences — Selling to Home Services Companies

## Infrastructure Setup

### Domains (Buy 3-5)
- uais-solutions.com
- uaiscale.com
- uais-systems.com
- uaispartners.com
- scalewithuais.com

### Warmup
- 2-3 weeks warmup per domain before sending
- Use Instantly.io or Smartlead warmup pools
- Start at 5 emails/day, ramp to 50/day per inbox
- Create 2-3 inboxes per domain
- Total capacity: 300-750 emails/day

### Email Verification
- Verify every email before sending (Apollo, NeverBounce, or ZeroBounce)
- Bounce rate must stay under 2%
- Remove catch-all domains or send at lower volume

### Volume Per Day (Target)
- Sequence 1: 150-300 emails/day (primary)
- Sequence 2: 50-100 emails/day (seasonal, HVAC only)
- Sequence 3: 25-50 emails/day (PM outreach)
- Sequence 4: 50-100 emails/day (review angle)
- **Total: 275-550 emails/day**

### Expected Results
- Open rate: 45-65% (personalized subject lines)
- Reply rate: 3-8%
- Positive reply rate: 1.5-4%
- Meeting book rate: 0.5-2% of total sends
- **At 400 emails/day: 2-8 meetings booked per day = 40-160 meetings/month**
