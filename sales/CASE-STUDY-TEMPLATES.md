## Case Study Production Checklist

### When to Create
- [ ] After 30 days of service (first results available)
- [ ] Get owner permission (verbal on monthly call, follow up with email)
- [ ] Offer incentive: "We'll feature you in our case study — free month of service"

### Data to Collect
- [ ] Missed calls before vs after
- [ ] Average response time before vs after
- [ ] Estimates sent vs closed before vs after
- [ ] Revenue recovered/added (actual numbers from their CRM)
- [ ] Google reviews before vs after (count + rating)
- [ ] Owner quote (record on call, transcribe)
- [ ] Company photo/logo for the case study

### Formats to Produce
- [ ] One-page PDF (for email attachments)
- [ ] Full website page (for SEO + credibility)
- [ ] LinkedIn post version (3-5 per case study)
- [ ] Facebook group post version
- [ ] Video testimonial (if owner is willing — 2-minute phone recording)
- [ ] Email sequence insert (reference in cold emails)

### Distribution
- [ ] Add to website case studies page
- [ ] Include in cold email sequences (Email 3 = social proof)
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn (stagger over 2-3 weeks)
- [ ] Share in Facebook contractor groups
- [ ] Include in sales deck/proposal
- [ ] Send to current clients (referral trigger)

## Goal: 2-3 New Case Studies Per Month

### Month 1-3: Build the First 5
- Offer first 5 clients discounted setup in exchange for case study rights
- Document EVERYTHING from day 1
- Use actual screenshots of data where possible

### Month 4-6: Scale to Industry Authority
- 10-15 published case studies
- At least 2 per trade (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical)
- Start getting video testimonials

### Month 7-12: Become the Obvious Choice
- 25+ case studies
- Segment by trade, company size, and market
- "We've done this 25 times in HVAC alone. Here are the results."
- Competitors can't match this level of proof
