## Step 1: FIND THEM — Building Your Prospect List

### Who We're Looking For (ICP)

**Perfect Fit:**
- HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical company
- $1M-$5M annual revenue (5-25 employees)
- Owner-operated (decision maker IS the owner)
- Active Google Business Profile with 50+ reviews
- Currently running Google Ads or Local Services Ads
- Located in metro areas (250K+ population)
- Has a website (even a basic one)

**Good Signals:**
- Yelp profile with mixed reviews (they need help)
- Phone goes to voicemail after hours (they're missing calls)
- Website has no chat or contact form (leads are leaking)
- Competitors in their area have stronger online presence
- They post on social media sporadically (want to market but no system)

**Red Flags (Avoid):**
- Solo operator with no employees (can't afford $497/mo)
- Brand new business (< 1 year, no revenue to recover)
- Already using Hatch or similar AI system
- Franchise with corporate-mandated systems
- Owner is tech-hostile (won't adopt anything new)

### Where to Find Them

**Source 1: Google Maps Scraping**
- Search: "[trade] companies in [city]"
- Filter for: 50+ reviews, website listed, phone number
- Tools: Apify Google Maps scraper, Outscraper, or manual
- Expected yield: 200-500 prospects per metro area

**Source 2: State Contractor License Registries**
- Every state has a public database of licensed contractors
- Filter by trade, license status, year established
- Cross-reference with Google Maps for contact info
- Gold mine for verified, legitimate businesses

**Source 3: Google Local Services Ads**
- Search for your target trade in target cities
- Every business running LSA is ALREADY spending on leads
- They understand marketing spend = perfect prospect
- Screenshot their LSA listing (use in outreach)

**Source 4: Yelp Business Pages**
- Filter by trade, city, review count
- Businesses with 3-4 star average are IDEAL (they need review help)
- Get owner name from Yelp business info

**Source 5: Facebook Groups**
- "HVAC Business Owners"
- "Contractor Business Growth"
- "[City] Home Services Professionals"
- Engage first, prospect second

**Source 6: Industry Directories**
- BBB (Better Business Bureau)
- Angi Pro network
- HomeAdvisor Pro network
- Trade association member lists (PHCC, ACCA, NRCA)

### Channel 2: Cold Call Script

**Opening (First 15 seconds — make or break):**
> "Hey [First Name], this is Justin. I'm not trying to sell you anything right now — I just have a quick question. Who answers your phones when your team is out on jobs?"

**If they engage:**
> "The reason I ask — I work with [trade] companies and we found that the average company your size is missing about 45 calls a month. At your average job value, that's $9,000-$15,000 in revenue that just disappears. We built an AI system that catches every single one of those calls. **If they say they don't miss calls:**
> "That's great — you'd be in the minority. Mind if I ask — what happens at 7pm on a Wednesday when a homeowner's pipe bursts and they call you? Does that go to voicemail or does someone pick up?"

**If they're busy:**
> "Totally get it — you're running a business. When's a better time for a 10-minute call? I just want to show you one number — how much revenue [Company Name] is losing to missed calls. If the number doesn't surprise you, I'll never bother you again."

## Step 3: SHOW THEM THE MONEY — The Demo/Audit Call

### Pre-Call Research (5 minutes)
Before every call, gather:
1. Their Google Business Profile (reviews, rating, response rate)
2. Their website (does it have chat? Contact form? Phone number prominent?)
3. Call their main number at 6pm — does anyone answer?
4. Their top 3 competitors in the area (compare reviews)
5. Are they running Google Ads / LSA?

### The Call Structure (15-20 minutes)

**Minute 0-3: Rapport + Context**
> "Hey [First Name], thanks for jumping on. Before I show you anything, tell me about [Company Name] — how long you been at it? How many guys do you have out in the field?"

**Minute 3-7: Pain Discovery**
> "Let me ask you a few quick questions..."
> 1. "What happens when a customer calls after hours right now?"
> 2. "When your guys finish a job, how do they ask for reviews?"
> 3. "When you send an estimate and the customer doesn't respond, what's your follow-up process?"
> 4. "How do you get new commercial accounts — property managers, realtors?"
> 5. "If I asked your team how many calls went to voicemail last week, would they know?"

**Minute 7-12: The Audit Reveal**
> "So here's what I found before this call..."
> - "I called your number at [time] — went to voicemail." [if true]
> - "Your Google profile has [X] reviews at [rating]. Your top competitor [Name] has [Y] reviews at [rating]."
> - "Based on companies your size, you're likely missing 35-50 calls/month. At your average job value of $[X], that's $[amount] in lost revenue."
> - "You sent roughly [X] estimates last month. If 40-60% went unfollowed, that's another $[amount] walking away."

**Minute 12-17: The Solution**
> "Here's what we do about it..."
> - Walk through Tier 1 (or Tier 2 if they're bigger)
> - Show exactly which problems it solves from THEIR audit
> - Reference a similar client's results
> - Make it tangible: "So instead of those 45 missed calls, our AI picks up every single one. Instead of estimates going cold, they get followed up automatically."

**Minute 17-20: The Close**
> "The setup is $1,500 and then $497/month. But here's the thing — if we don't book you at least 10 additional jobs in the first 30 days from leads you're already missing, I refund the setup fee. So there's literally no risk."
>
> "We can have this live in 7 days. Want to get started?"

### If They Need to Think About It
> "Totally understand. Here's what I'd suggest — let me run a full 7-day call audit for you. Free. I'll track every call that comes into [Company Name] for a week and show you exactly how many are being missed. Then you can decide with real data. Fair?"

## Step 4: CLOSE — Removing All Friction

### Closing Techniques

**The Calculator Close:**
Show them the math on a shared screen:
- Missed calls/month x average job value = revenue lost
- Cost of our system/month = tiny fraction of revenue recovered
- "You're paying $497 to recover $5,000-$15,000. This is a no-brainer."

**The Competitor Close:**
> "Your competitor [Name] is going to figure this out eventually. The question is whether you're the one answering at 9pm or they are. Whoever picks up first wins 78% of the time."

**The Risk Reversal Close:**
> "Look — worst case scenario, we don't hit 10 jobs in 30 days and you get your money back. Best case, you're looking at $5K-$15K in recovered revenue every month for the rest of your business. What's the downside?"

**The Urgency Close (Genuine):**
> "I can only onboard 4 new clients per month because we custom-build every system. I've got 2 spots left this month. If you want to lock in [month], I need a yes by [date]."

### Payment Structure
- Setup fee: Collected at signing (Stripe invoice)
- Monthly: Auto-charge on the 1st of each month
- Contract: Month-to-month (no long-term lock-in)
- Guarantee: 30-day money-back on setup if we don't hit 10 additional jobs
