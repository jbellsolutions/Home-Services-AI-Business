# Objection Handling Guide

## Every Objection You'll Hear and Exactly How to Respond

---

## PRICE OBJECTIONS

### "That's too expensive"
> "I hear you. Let me ask — how much are you spending on Google Ads or LSA right now?"
> [They say $2K-$15K/month]
> "So you're spending $[X]/month to GENERATE leads. Our system costs $497/month to CAPTURE the leads you're already paying for but missing. You're spending $[X] to ring the phone, and then nobody picks up. We fix the second half."

### "$497/month adds up"
> "It does — $5,964/year. But let's look at the other number: you're losing $9,000-$15,000 every single month in missed calls alone. That's $108,000-$180,000/year. I'm asking you to spend $6K to recover $100K+. Would you spend a dollar to make twenty?"

### "I can't afford it right now"
> "When you say you can't afford it — is that because cash flow is tight? Because here's the thing: the reason cash flow is tight might be BECAUSE you're missing leads. Our system typically pays for itself in the first week. Would it help if we did a smaller setup fee spread over 2 months?"

### "My buddy knows a guy who does it cheaper"
> "There's always someone cheaper. The question is: do they specialize in [trade] companies? Do they integrate with ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro? Do they guarantee results? We've built this specifically for [trade] — every sequence, every script, every automation is designed for your business. That's why it works."

### "Can you do it for less?"
> "Here's what I can do: the pricing is the pricing because it reflects what it costs to deliver results. But I'll make this deal — if we don't hit 10 additional jobs in 30 days, I'll refund the setup fee AND give you the second month free. I'm betting on our system with my own money. Fair?"

## TRUST/SKEPTICISM OBJECTIONS

### "I've been burned before by marketing companies"
> "Yeah, and I'm guessing they sold you leads or promised rankings and then disappeared. We're different — we don't generate leads. You're ALREADY generating leads. We just make sure you actually capture them. And we show you exactly how many calls we answer, leads we convert, and revenue we recover every single month. If the numbers don't work, you fire us. No contract."

### "How do I know this actually works?"
> "Fair question. Here's what I'd suggest: let me run a free 7-day call audit. I'll track every call that comes into [Company Name] for a week and show you exactly how many go unanswered. No commitment, no cost. If the number doesn't shock you, we shake hands and move on. If it does, we talk about fixing it."

### "I don't trust AI to talk to my customers"
> "That's the #1 concern every owner has, and I respect it. Here's the reality: the AI doesn't replace your customer service — it's the first touch. It answers the call, captures the info, books the appointment, and THEN your team takes over for the actual service. Think of it as a really good receptionist who never misses a call, never calls in sick, and works 24/7. And if a customer ever wants to talk to a human, it transfers immediately."

### "What if the AI says something wrong?"
> "We build in a quality gauntlet — every outgoing message is checked against your service menu, pricing, and policies. The AI can't quote prices you haven't approved or promise services you don't offer. And we monitor call recordings weekly. If something's off, we catch it fast and fix it. Plus, you have a dashboard where you can see every interaction."

### "This sounds too good to be true"
> "I get that. Here's the math: you miss 45 calls/month. The AI answers them. Some of those calls become jobs. At $350 average, even 10 extra jobs = $3,500/month. You're paying $497. The math isn't magic — it's just fixing a hole in your bucket that's been there all along."

## TIMING OBJECTIONS

### "Not the right time"
> "When IS the right time? [If it's winter for HVAC]: 'Summer is your money season. If we set up now, you're fully dialed in before the rush hits. Every company that waits until June to fix their systems is scrambling while their competitor is already booked out.' [If it's summer]: 'You're in peak season right now — which means every missed call is costing you maximum money. This is literally the MOST expensive time to NOT have this.'"

### "Let me think about it"
> "Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through? Is it the price, whether it'll work, or something else? [Let them answer, then address the real objection]"
>
> Alternative: "Here's what I'd suggest — instead of thinking about it, let me run the free call audit. You'll have REAL data in 7 days, and then you can make a decision based on numbers, not guesses."

### "Call me back next quarter"
> "Happy to. Quick question though — how many calls will [Company Name] miss between now and next quarter? At 45/month, that's 135 missed calls. At $350 average, that's $47,250 in revenue that walks away while you wait. What if we just got the basic system running now and you're capturing those calls during Q[next]?"

### "We're in our slow season"
> "That's actually the BEST time to set this up. You're not scrambling. Your team can learn the system without pressure. And when the busy season hits, you're already dialed in while your competitors are still figuring it out. Every client who set up during slow season told me it was the best decision they made."

## COMPETITION/ALTERNATIVE OBJECTIONS

### "We already have a CRM/ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro"
> "Perfect — we plug INTO those systems, not replace them. ServiceTitan is great for managing jobs. But who answers the phone at 9pm? Who follows up on the 60 estimates that went cold? Who asks for reviews automatically? That's what we do. We make your existing CRM actually work harder."

### "We already use an answering service"
> "How's that working? [They usually say: 'okay, but...'] The difference is an answering service takes a message. Our AI books an appointment, sends a follow-up text, adds them to your CRM, and starts a nurture sequence — all in under 60 seconds. An answering service costs $200-$400/month and gives you a sticky note. We cost $497/month and give you booked jobs."

### "My office manager handles all of that"
> "What happens when she's on the other line? On lunch? Out sick? On vacation? Or after 5pm? 40% of home services calls come outside business hours. And can she follow up on 50 estimates simultaneously, post 3 Google reviews per week, and send seasonal campaigns to 500 past customers? I'm not replacing her — I'm giving her superpowers."

### "I saw [competitor product] — what makes you different?"
> "Most of those tools do ONE thing — they might do texting or review management. We do the full stack: phone, text, email, reviews, estimates, seasonal campaigns — and it's all built specifically for [trade] companies. We integrate with your CRM, we know your seasonal patterns, we know what messaging works for [trade]. It's not generic AI — it's AI trained on how [trade] businesses actually work."

## KNOWLEDGE/UNDERSTANDING OBJECTIONS

### "I don't understand AI / I'm not tech-savvy"
> "You don't need to be. Honestly, after we set it up, you don't touch anything. You get a weekly report showing: 'This many calls answered, this many jobs booked, this much revenue recovered.' That's it. Your phone rings, the AI answers, jobs show up on your calendar. The tech part is our problem."

### "My customers want to talk to a real person"
> "Absolutely — and they will. The AI handles the first touch: 'Hi, thanks for calling [Company Name]. I can help you schedule a service appointment right now. What's the issue you're experiencing?' It books the appointment, and then YOUR team shows up in person. The customer gets faster service, and you don't miss the call. If they insist on a human, it transfers to your cell immediately."

### "How does it work with my phone system?"
> "We can work with whatever you have. If you use a regular business line, we set up call forwarding. If you use VoIP, we integrate directly. If you use your cell phone, we can route through that too. We've worked with every setup from a single cell phone to multi-location phone systems."

## STATUS QUO OBJECTIONS

### "We're doing fine without it"
> "That's great — you've built a solid business. But 'fine' and 'optimized' are different things. Let me ask: if you found out you had a $10,000/month leak in your business, would you want to know about it? That's what I'm offering to show you. Free audit, no commitment."

### "Word of mouth is working for us"
> "Word of mouth is awesome — it's also unpredictable. You can't control when referrals come in, and you can't scale it. What happens when you want to grow from $2M to $5M? Word of mouth can't get you there alone. We're not replacing word of mouth — we're building a system that runs alongside it so you're never dependent on one source."

### "I've been doing this for 20 years without AI"
> "And you've built something real — I respect that. But think about this: the company down the street that started 2 years ago? They've got AI answering every call, following up on every estimate, and getting reviews on autopilot. They're growing faster than companies that have been around 20 years. The trade hasn't changed — but the business of the trade has. This is the new normal."

## THE POWER MOVE: THE FREE AUDIT

When in doubt on any objection, fall back to the free audit:

> "Tell you what — forget everything I just said. Let me do one thing for you for free. I'll track every call that comes into [Company Name] for 7 days. At the end, I'll show you a report: how many calls came in, how many were answered, how many went to voicemail, and what that's costing you in revenue. If the number is small, great — you're doing better than most. If it's not, we'll talk. Zero cost, zero commitment. Fair enough?"

**This works because:**
1. It removes ALL risk from their decision
2. It lets the DATA do the selling
3. It creates a natural follow-up conversation
4. Nobody says no to free
5. The results are ALWAYS eye-opening (62% miss rate is real)

**Conversion rate from free audit to paid client: 60-70%**
