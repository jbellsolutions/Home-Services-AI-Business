# Marketplace & Platform Lead Gen Channels

## Every Platform Ranked: Cost, Quality, Volume, and Verdict

## TIER 1: MUST-HAVE CHANNELS (High ROI, Start Here)

---

### 1. Google Local Services Ads (LSA)

**How it works:** Pay-per-lead ads that appear at the very top of Google search results with a "Google Guaranteed" or "Google Screened" badge. Customers call or message directly from the ad.

**Cost Structure:**
- Pay per lead (not per click)
- Cost per lead: $25-$150 depending on trade and market
- HVAC: $30-$75/lead
- Plumbing: $25-$60/lead
- Roofing: $50-$150/lead
- Electrical: $25-$65/lead
- No minimum spend — set your own weekly budget
- Dispute invalid leads for credit

**Lead Quality: 9/10**
- These are people actively searching for your service RIGHT NOW
- Pre-qualified: they see your rating, reviews, and service area before calling
- 31% average conversion rate from lead to booked job
- Effective cost per booked job: ~$100-$250 (best in the industry)

**Volume:** High — Google processes 8.5B searches/day. LSA captures the highest-intent traffic.

**Effort to Maintain:** Medium
- Requires: background check, insurance verification, license verification
- Ongoing: respond to leads quickly (affects ranking), manage reviews, adjust budget
- Time: 30-60 min/week

**Verdict: ESSENTIAL.** If you do nothing else, run LSA. It's the single highest-ROI lead channel for home services. Period.

**Setup Requirements:**
1. Google Business Profile (verified, optimized)
2. Background check (through Google's provider)
3. Insurance verification
4. License verification
5. Minimum 10+ Google reviews recommended (more = better ranking)

### 2. Google Business Profile (Free)

**How it works:** Your free business listing on Google Maps and local search. Shows up when people search "[trade] near me."

**Cost: $0**

**Lead Quality: 8/10**
- High-intent searchers
- Directly drives phone calls, website visits, and direction requests
- Reviews are the #1 factor in local purchase decisions

**Volume:** High — appears in "Local Pack" (the map section) for relevant searches

**Effort:** Low-Medium
- Initial setup: 1-2 hours
- Ongoing: post weekly, respond to reviews, update photos
- Time: 15-30 min/week

**Verdict: ESSENTIAL.** Free, high-impact, and the foundation for LSA and local SEO. Every contractor must have an optimized GBP.

**Optimization Checklist:**
- [ ] Complete every field (hours, services, description, attributes)
- [ ] 50+ photos (before/after, team, equipment, completed projects)
- [ ] Post weekly (offers, tips, completed projects)
- [ ] Respond to every review within 24 hours
- [ ] Enable messaging
- [ ] Add all service categories
- [ ] Verify service area

### 3. Nextdoor

**How it works:** Hyper-local social network organized by verified neighborhoods. 77% of users are homeowners. Contractors can create free business pages and run targeted ads.

**Cost Structure:**
- Free business page (claim and optimize)
- Paid ads: ~$20 CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions)
- Budget: $100-$500/month for meaningful reach
- Free: Neighborhood recommendations from satisfied customers

**Lead Quality: 8/10**
- 77% homeowners (exact target audience)
- Users 161% more likely to be interested in remodeling/construction
- 67% share service provider recommendations
- 94% trust rate — people trust neighbor recommendations
- Reported close rates: up to 44%

**Volume:** Medium-High — depends on neighborhood density

**Effort:** Low
- Setup: 30 minutes
- Ongoing: respond to recommendations, occasional post
- Time: 15 min/week

**Verdict: HIGHLY RECOMMENDED.** Massively underused by contractors. The trust factor and homeowner concentration make this a goldmine. Free tier alone generates leads from recommendations.

**Strategy:**
1. Claim your business page
2. Ask satisfied customers to recommend you on Nextdoor
3. Engage in neighborhood posts about home services
4. Run targeted ads during seasonal peaks
5. Post helpful tips (not pitches) to build authority

## TIER 2: VALUABLE ADD-ONS (Good ROI When Used Right)

---

### 4. Craigslist

**See: [CRAIGSLIST-SYSTEM.md](./CRAIGSLIST-SYSTEM.md) for the complete deep-dive**

**Quick Summary:**
- Cost: $7-10/post
- Lead quality: 6/10 (price-conscious but high-intent)
- Volume: 5-15 leads/month per metro area
- ROI: 5-12x
- Best for: supplemental leads, entry-point offers, emergency services
- **Worth it?** Yes, as a low-cost supplement. Not as a primary channel.

### 5. Facebook (Ads, NOT Marketplace)

**How it works:** Run targeted ads to homeowners in your service area. Facebook Marketplace technically doesn't allow service listings, but your business page and ads reach the same audience.

**Cost Structure:**
- Facebook Ads: $15-$50 per lead (varies by trade and market)
- Marketplace: Free (but service posts get removed — use carefully)
- Minimum ad budget: $10-$20/day to test, $500-$1,500/month to scale

**Lead Quality: 6/10**
- Lower intent than Google (people aren't actively searching)
- But targetable: homeowners, income level, zip code, interests
- Good for awareness and retargeting
- Leads need more nurturing to convert

**Volume:** Very High — 2.9B monthly active users

**Effort:** Medium-High
- Requires: ad creative (images/video), copy, landing pages
- Ongoing: monitor ad performance, adjust targeting, refresh creative
- Time: 2-5 hours/week (or hire it out)

**Verdict: GOOD for established businesses** with budget for proper ad campaigns. Not ideal for beginners who need quick, cheap leads. Works best for seasonal pushes and brand building.

**Facebook Marketplace Hack:**
While you can't officially post services, some contractors:
1. Post items related to their trade (free removal of old equipment)
2. Respond to "ISO" (in search of) posts in local groups
3. Use their personal profile to share deals (not as a business page)
4. Note: This is gray area — accounts can get restricted

### 6. Yelp

**How it works:** Free business listing + optional paid advertising. Consumers use Yelp to research and find local services.

**Cost Structure:**
- Free listing: $0
- Yelp Ads: $150-$1,000+/month (based on market/competition)
- Yelp charges per click or per impression depending on ad type
- "Yelp Guaranteed" program: similar to Google Guaranteed

**Lead Quality: 7/10**
- Yelp users are research-heavy — they read reviews carefully
- Higher consideration but slower decision cycle
- Yelp's review system is trusted but aggressive (filters many reviews)

**Volume:** Medium — depends on market. Stronger in urban areas.

**Effort:** Low (free tier) / Medium (paid)
- Setup: 30 minutes
- Ongoing: respond to reviews, keep info updated
- Time: 15 min/week (free) / 1 hour/week (paid)

**Verdict: Maintain a free presence.** Respond to all reviews. Paid Yelp ads are overpriced for most contractors — spend that money on LSA instead. But a strong free Yelp profile with good reviews adds credibility.

### 7. Home Depot Pro Referral

**How it works:** Home Depot refers customers who buy products in-store to vetted local contractors for installation. Contractors earn "referral credits" (points) rather than paying per lead.

**Cost Structure:**
- No upfront cost
- Points-based referral system (essentially free leads in exchange for being in their network)
- Must meet Home Depot's qualification requirements

**Lead Quality: 7/10**
- Customers have already purchased the product (high intent)
- They need installation NOW
- Pre-qualified buyer (already spent money at HD)

**Volume:** Low-Medium — depends on your trade and local HD volume

**Effort:** Low
- Application and qualification process
- Maintain good ratings with HD customers
- Time: minimal ongoing effort

**Verdict: FREE leads with zero risk.** Apply and get in the network. The volume isn't huge, but the leads are pre-qualified buyers who need immediate help. No reason NOT to do this.

**Requirements:**
- Licensed and insured
- Background check
- Customer service commitment
- Available for timely installation

### 8. Porch.com

**How it works:** Porch partners with Lowe's and connects homeowners with contractors. Leads come from both the Porch website and Lowe's in-store referrals.

**Cost Structure:**
- Free listing available
- Porch Pro (paid): varies by trade — $15-$100+ per lead
- Leads are shared (not exclusive) on free tier
- Exclusive leads cost more

**Lead Quality: 6/10**
- Lowe's partnership gives some pre-qualification
- But shared leads mean you're competing with 3-5 other contractors per lead
- Mixed reviews from contractors on lead quality

**Volume:** Medium

**Effort:** Low
- Setup: 30 minutes
- Ongoing: respond to leads quickly, maintain profile
- Time: 15 min/week

**Verdict: Worth the free tier.** Claim your profile, add reviews and photos. Paid leads are hit-or-miss — test with a small budget before committing.

## TIER 3: USE WITH CAUTION (Declining ROI or High Risk)

---

### 9. Thumbtack

**How it works:** Homeowners post projects, contractors bid on them. You pay for each lead (whether or not they respond to you).

**Cost Structure:**
- Pay per lead: $15-$100+ (varies dramatically by trade and project size)
- No subscription fee
- You pay when you send a quote (even if customer never responds)
- Prices have been rising steadily

**Lead Quality: 4/10 (declining)**
- Significant reports of fake/unresponsive leads
- Price shoppers dominate the platform
- You're competing directly with other contractors who bid lower
- Race to the bottom on pricing

**Volume:** Medium-High (lots of leads, but quality is poor)

**Effort:** Medium
- Must respond quickly to win bids
- Custom quotes for each lead
- Time: 1-2 hours/day to stay competitive

**Verdict: DECLINING.** Was good 3-5 years ago. Quality has deteriorated significantly. High cost for low-quality, shared leads. Most experienced contractors have moved away from Thumbtack. Only use if you're brand new and need ANY leads to get started.

---

### 10. Angi (formerly HomeAdvisor)

**How it works:** Homeowners request service, Angi sells the lead to 3-4 contractors simultaneously. You pay per lead regardless of outcome.

**Cost Structure:**
- Annual membership: $300-$500/year
- Pay per lead: $15-$80+ on top of membership
- Leads are shared with 3-4 competitors
- Aggressive contract terms (hard to cancel)
- Some contractors report effective cost per booked job: $800-$1,400+

**Lead Quality: 5/10**
- Shared leads = you're one of 4 contractors calling
- Speed to respond is everything (first call wins)
- Some fake/low-quality leads reported
- Aggressive sales team pushes expensive add-ons

**Volume:** High (they have a massive homeowner base)

**Effort:** Medium
- Must respond to leads within minutes
- Follow up aggressively
- Time: 1-2 hours/day

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION.** The math often doesn't work — paying $300/year membership + $50/lead for shared leads that 3 other contractors also received is expensive. Can work if you're extremely fast on follow-up and your close rate is high. But there are better uses of that money (LSA, Nextdoor, Craigslist).

---

### 11. Houzz

**How it works:** Design/renovation platform where homeowners find inspiration and contractors. Strong for kitchen/bath remodeling, landscaping, and higher-end work.

**Cost Structure:**
- Free profile with portfolio
- Houzz Pro: $65-$499+/month (CRM + marketing tools)
- Lead generation add-on: $499+/month

**Lead Quality: 7/10 (for the right trades)**
- Higher-end homeowners planning renovation projects
- Longer sales cycle but bigger job values ($10K-$100K+)
- Good for: kitchen/bath remodel, landscaping, painting, flooring

**Volume:** Low (quality over quantity)

**Effort:** Medium
- Build portfolio with project photos
- Write descriptions, tag materials/styles
- Time: 1-2 hours/month to maintain

**Verdict: FREE PROFILE IS WORTH IT** for renovation/remodel trades. Upload your best project photos. The paid lead gen is overpriced and low-volume — skip it. But a strong Houzz portfolio drives credibility.

---

### 12. BuildZoom

**How it works:** Matches homeowners with licensed contractors. BuildZoom verifies licenses, pulls permit history, and ranks contractors by quality.

**Cost Structure:**
- Free to list
- 2.5% commission ONLY if you're hired through the platform
- No upfront cost, no lead fees

**Lead Quality: 7/10**
- Pre-qualified homeowners with real projects
- License verification adds trust
- Commission-only model means zero risk

**Volume:** Low-Medium

**Verdict: ZERO-RISK ADD-ON.** Free to list, only pay if you get hired. No reason not to be on it.

## THE RECOMMENDED STACK (Priority Order)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2)
1. **Google Business Profile** — Optimize fully, start collecting reviews
2. **Google LSA** — Apply, get verified, start running ads
3. **Nextdoor** — Claim business page, ask for recommendations

### Phase 2: Supplement (Week 3-4)
4. **Craigslist** — Launch posting system (see CRAIGSLIST-SYSTEM.md)
5. **Yelp** — Claim free listing, optimize profile
6. **Home Depot Pro** — Apply to referral program
7. **BuildZoom** — Create free listing

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 2+)
8. **Facebook Ads** — Test with $500/month budget
9. **Porch** — Claim free profile
10. **Houzz** — Upload project portfolio (if renovation trade)

### Phase 4: Evaluate (Month 3+)
11. Review ROI from each channel
12. Double down on winners
13. Drop or reduce losers
14. Consider Thumbtack/Angi ONLY if you need volume and can respond fast
