| Channel | Role | Volume | Cost | Speed to Results |
|---------|------|--------|------|-----------------|
| **Cold Email** | Primary volume driver | 40-100 meetings/mo | Low ($200-400/mo) | 2-4 weeks |

**Total capacity: 85-210 meetings/month**
**At 40% close rate on 50% show rate: 17-42 new clients/month**

## Channel 2: CRAIGSLIST + MARKETPLACE PLATFORMS

**See: [CRAIGSLIST-SYSTEM.md](./CRAIGSLIST-SYSTEM.md) and [MARKETPLACE-CHANNELS.md](./MARKETPLACE-CHANNELS.md)**

### The Two-Sided Strategy

**Side A: Selling OUR services to contractors (Finding Clients)**
Post ads offering our AI system to contractors:
- Category: "business services" or "computer services"
- Target: contractor business owners looking for marketing help
- Example ad: "HVAC Companies — Stop Missing Calls. AI System Books Jobs 24/7"
- This is a lower-volume but zero-competition channel for finding clients

**Side B: Generating leads FOR our clients (Proving Our Value)**
Once we have clients, we post ads on their behalf as part of the Tier 2 service:
- Category: "skilled trade services"
- Run the full Craigslist system for each client
- Track and attribute leads
- This becomes a deliverable we provide AND a case study for selling to new clients

### Active Platforms for Finding OUR Clients
1. **Craigslist** — business services section
2. **Facebook Groups** — contractor business owner groups
3. **LinkedIn** — HVAC/plumbing business owner connections
4. **Nextdoor** — engage with homeowner complaints about contractors (then reach out to those contractors)
5. **Google Business Profiles** — find contractors with low reviews/poor response (they need us)

### Cost: $100-$500/month total

## Channel 5: PARTNERSHIPS

### Partner Types

**A. HVAC/Trade Distributors**
- Carrier, Trane, Lennox dealer networks
- They want their dealers to succeed (sell more equipment)
- Offer: "We help your dealers book more install jobs"
- Model: Revenue share or co-marketing

**B. Industry Consultants/Coaches**
- HVAC business coaches, contractor consultants
- They already have the audience and trust
- Offer: White-label our system or referral fee
- Model: $500-$1,000 per referred client

**C. Marketing Agencies Serving Home Services**
- SEO agencies, PPC agencies, web designers for contractors
- They generate leads but can't help with lead CAPTURE
- Offer: "Your clients are wasting the leads you generate. We fix that."
- Model: Referral fee or white-label partnership

**D. Software Vendors**
- ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber
- Integration partnerships
- "We're a ServiceTitan partner that helps your users maximize the platform"
- Model: Co-marketing, integration directory listing

**E. Trade Associations**
- ACCA (Air Conditioning Contractors of America)
- PHCC (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors Association)
- NRCA (National Roofing Contractors Association)
- Offer: Speak at events, sponsor newsletters, member discounts
- Model: Bulk deals for association members

### Referral Program
- $500 cash per referred client that closes
- Open to: current clients, partners, anyone
- Tracking via unique referral codes
- Monthly referral leaderboard

### Cost: Variable (mostly time + referral payouts)
### Expected: 5-10 meetings/month (after month 6)

## THE ONE-PAGE SUMMARY

**How we get clients:**
1. **Cold email** thousands of contractor owners per week with personalized outreach showing them how much revenue they're losing
2. **Post on Craigslist** and marketplace platforms to catch contractors actively looking for help
3. **Engage on LinkedIn and Facebook** to build relationships with trade business owners
4. **Create content** that positions us as the authority on AI for home services
5. **Partner** with distributors, consultants, and agencies who already serve contractors
6. **Run ads** targeting contractor business owners who are already spending on marketing

**The killer offer that makes it all work:**
> "We'll track every call to your business for 7 days — free. We'll show you exactly how many calls you're missing and what it's costing you. If the number doesn't shock you, we shake hands and move on."

**Nobody says no to free data. And the data ALWAYS sells the system.**
