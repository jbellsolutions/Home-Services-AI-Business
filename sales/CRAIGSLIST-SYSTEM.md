# The Craigslist Lead Generation System

## A Complete Plug-and-Play System for Home Services Contractors

---

## Why Craigslist Still Works (And How to Use It Right)

Craigslist is NOT your primary lead channel — it's a **low-cost supplemental engine** that generates 5-15 additional leads/month at $7-10 per post. The audience is price-conscious but high-intent: people browsing CL services sections are actively looking for help right now.

**Best use case:** Entry-point offers (free estimates, seasonal specials, emergency service ads) that get customers in the door, then upsell into larger jobs.

**Not ideal for:** Premium/luxury services. CL has a "garage sale mentality" — expect budget-conscious buyers.

---

## The Rules (Break These and You're Ghosted)

### Posting Limits
| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Posts per category per city | 1 every 48 hours |
| Total posts per day (recommended) | 2 max (different categories/areas) |
| Renew existing posts | Every 48 hours (bumps to top) |
| Phone numbers per account | 1 at a time |
| Phone numbers tried per 12 hours | 3 max |
| Phone number unlinking | After 90 days |

### What Gets You Ghosted (Silently Hidden)
1. Duplicate content (same title, body, or photos across posts)
2. Posting same ad within 48 hours
3. Wrong category selection
4. IP address doesn't match the target city
5. Brand new account posting aggressively
6. Reusing old post text verbatim
7. Too many external links

### What Gets You Flagged (Removed by Users)
1. ALL CAPS titles
2. Excessive symbols/emojis
3. No contact information
4. Spammy/template-looking ads
5. Misleading claims
6. Ads that look like mass marketing

### Phone Verification Requirements
- VOIP numbers NOT accepted — must be a real US/CA/AU/UK number
- Voice call or text verification
- One phone per account
- Cannot link to more than one CL account at a time

## Where to Post

### Primary Category: Skilled Trade Services ("sks")
This is where homeowners search for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical. This is your main posting target.

### Secondary Category: Household Services
Good for overlap services — handyman, general repairs, cleaning, landscaping.

### Do NOT Use: Gigs Section
The gigs section is for freelance/one-off labor — it attracts a completely different audience. Use it for recruiting helpers/subcontractors, not for advertising your services.

## Photo Strategy (Critical for Conversions)

### What to Include (4-8 photos per ad)
1. **Before/After shots** — The #1 converting image type for contractors
2. **Your branded truck/van** — Builds legitimacy
3. **Your team in uniform** — Shows professionalism
4. **Completed project closeups** — Quality craftsmanship
5. **Equipment/tools** — Shows you're legit and equipped
6. **Google review screenshot** — Social proof in image form

### Photo Rules
- Optimal size: 600 x 450 pixels
- Use UNIQUE photos per ad variant (reusing triggers ghosting)
- No stock photos — they look fake and get flagged
- Include your logo/branding subtly in photos

## Multi-City Strategy

### If You Serve 1 Metro Area
- 1 CL account, 1 verified phone number
- Post every 48 hours in "skilled trade services"
- Rotate through 5 ad templates
- Budget: ~$105-$150/month

### If You Serve 3-5 Metro Areas

**Setup:**
- 1 CL account per metro area
- 1 unique phone number per account
- Unique ad content per metro (different titles, body, photos)
- Residential proxy or IP matching each metro area (optional but recommended)

**Posting Schedule:**
| Day | Metro A | Metro B | Metro C |
|-----|---------|---------|---------|
| Mon | Post Template 1 | Renew | Post Template 3 |
| Tue | — | Post Template 2 | — |
| Wed | Renew | — | Renew |
| Thu | Post Template 2 | Renew | Post Template 1 |
| Fri | — | Post Template 4 | — |
| Sat | Renew | — | Renew |
| Sun | — | — | — |

**Content Rotation:**
- Never post the same template in the same city within 2 weeks
- Localize each ad (city name, neighborhood references, local competitor mentions)
- Unique photos per metro area where possible

## Lead Tracking System

### Dedicated Tracking Numbers
Assign a unique phone number (via CallRail, Twilio, or Google Voice) to each:
- Metro area
- Ad template
- Time period (monthly)

This lets you measure exactly which ads, in which cities, generate the most leads.

|------|------|----------|--------------|-------|-------|--------|-------------|---------|------|-----|
| | | | | | | | | | | |

### Promo Codes
Include in ads: "Mention code CL-[CITY]-[MONTH] for 10% off"
Tracks which specific ads drive business.

### CRM Tagging
When leads come in, tag as "Craigslist - [City]" in your CRM to measure:
- Cost per lead from CL
- CL lead-to-booking conversion rate
- Average job value from CL leads
- Which cities/templates perform best

### Critical: Response Time
**CL leads go cold FAST.** These are people actively searching — if you don't respond in 5-10 minutes, they've already called 3 other contractors.

This is where our AI system shines: instant text-back on missed calls + immediate follow-up = dramatically higher close rate on CL leads vs. competitors who call back hours later.

## Automation Approach (Safe Method)

### Why NOT to Use Posting Bots
- Violates CL Terms of Use
- Account bans are permanent
- Ghosting is silent — you waste money thinking ads are live when nobody sees them

### The "Semi-Automated" Workflow (Recommended)

**Weekly (15-20 minutes):**
1. Open your Craigslist posting schedule spreadsheet
2. Select today's template for each metro area
3. Customize the template with localized details
4. Post manually (takes 3-5 minutes per post)
5. Log the post in your tracking sheet
6. Set a reminder to renew in 48 hours

**Monthly (30 minutes):**
1. Review tracking data — which templates/cities performed best
2. Retire low-performing templates
3. Create 1-2 new template variations based on what's working
4. Adjust metro area allocation based on ROI
5. Update seasonal offers

### Tools to Streamline (Without Violating TOS)
- **Text Expander** — Store ad templates for quick copy-paste
- **Canva** — Prepare ad images in CL-optimized sizes
- **Google Calendar** — Set recurring 48-hour reminders to renew/post
- **Google Sheets** — Tracking spreadsheet with formulas
- **CallRail/Twilio** — Tracking numbers per city/template
