## Ground Rules

### Communication
- Daily text or quick check-in during active weeks
- Weekly call to review numbers and plan ahead
- If something's not working, we say it

### Decision Making
- Marketing decisions: Operator leads, Partner informed
- Field/hiring decisions: Partner leads, Operator informed
- Major decisions (over $[1,000], new services, partnerships): Both agree

### If It Doesn't Work Out
- **First 30 days:** Either party can walk. No penalties
- **After 30 days:** 14 days written notice to exit
- **Operator exits:** Partner keeps the business, all accounts, all customer relationships. Operator transfers business-specific accounts within 14 days. Operator removes their systems
- **Partner exits:** Operator keeps marketing systems and templates. Business accounts stay with Partner's LLC
- **No non-compete:** Neither party is restricted afterward
- **Outstanding revenue:** Anything earned before exit still splits per the agreement

### Intellectual Property
- Marketing systems, AI tools, automation: Operator owns (used across multiple ventures)
- Business name, brand, customer list: Partner's LLC owns
- Ad copy and content for this business: Shared — both can use
