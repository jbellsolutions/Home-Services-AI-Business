## What It Does

**Gets you leads.** Posts your business on Craigslist and Facebook every single day. People looking for your service see you first.

**Answers your phone — even at 2am.** An AI picks up every call, figures out what they need, and books the appointment. No more missed calls. No more losing a job because you were on a ladder.

**Follows up so you don't have to.** Most customers need 2-3 contacts before they book. The system sends the texts and emails automatically. You just show up to the job.

**Asks for reviews.** After the job is done, it automatically asks happy customers to leave a Google review. More reviews = more calls.

**Keeps your name out there.** It handles your outreach to property managers, real estate agents, and other businesses that can send you steady work.

## Go-to-Market: Building Something You Actually Own

Most businesses are trapped renting their growth — paying for ads that stop the moment you stop paying, or building audiences on platforms that can cut your reach overnight.

This system is built around four pillars that grow your business on assets *you* own and control. No platform can take them away.

### Pillar 1 — Database Reactivation
You already have customers who haven't called in a while. That's a revenue source most businesses leave completely untapped. We build the campaigns that wake up your cold contacts and turn them back into paying customers.

### Pillar 2 — Owned Audiences
Email lists, SMS, newsletters — channels you control. When you send a message, it gets delivered. No algorithm deciding who sees it. We build the architecture and the content engine that keeps your name in front of people who already trust you.

### Pillar 3 — JV & Power Partnerships
Zero-cost growth through the right relationships. Property managers, real estate agents, general contractors, home inspectors — anyone who talks to homeowners before you do is a referral partner waiting to happen. We map every opportunity in your orbit and build the outreach playbook.

### Pillar 4 — Earned Media
Getting featured in local news, appearing on podcasts, showing up on YouTube — these build authority that compounds over time and converts better than any paid ad. One good piece of earned content turns into 52 pieces of distribution across platforms.
