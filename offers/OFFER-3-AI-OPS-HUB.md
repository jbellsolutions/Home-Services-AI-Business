#### 1. AI DISPATCHER -- Smart Job Routing

The AI Dispatcher replaces your whiteboard, your gut feeling, and the 45 minutes you spend every morning figuring out who goes where.

**How it works:**
- Every new job is automatically scored on: urgency, job type, location, estimated duration, and required skills
- The AI evaluates every available tech against: current location, drive time, skill certifications, job history with that customer, hours worked this week, and revenue quota
- Jobs are assigned and the tech gets a push notification with full job details, directions, and customer history
- If a tech runs late, the AI automatically adjusts the schedule and notifies affected customers
- Emergency calls are routed to the nearest available tech with the right certifications, within 90 seconds
- Customer gets auto-text: "Your technician [Name] is on the way. ETA: [time]. Track here: [link]"

**What this replaces:**
- Morning dispatch meetings (saves 30-45 min/day)
- Owner/manager making routing decisions all day
- Techs calling the office 8-15 times a day asking "where am I going next?"
- Customers waiting because nobody assigned their job

| Customer history with tech | 10% | Served this customer before (higher satisfaction) |
| Weekly hours/revenue balance | 10% | Tech needs 6 more billable hours this week |
| Customer urgency level | 5% | Emergency vs routine maintenance |

**Expected impact:**
