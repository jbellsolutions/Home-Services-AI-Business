## Pricing Psychology

### Why These Prices Work

**Tier 1 at $497/mo:**
- Below the "needs a committee decision" threshold for most small businesses
- Owner can decide alone, today
- Anchored against: answering service ($300-$800/mo) + review tool ($300-$500/mo) = $600-$1,300/mo for less functionality
- ROI story: "Pay $497, recover $5,000-$15,000. That's 10-30x."
- **The "no brainer" price point**

**Tier 2 at $997/mo:**
- Still owner-decidable for $1-3M companies
- Anchored against: marketing agency ($2,000-$5,000/mo) + SDR ($4,500/mo) = $6,500-$9,500/mo
- Price gap from T1 = $500/mo, but value gap is massive (3-5x more revenue)
- **The "growth" price point — if T1 is working, this is obvious**

**Tier 3 at $1,997/mo:**
- For $3M+ companies — still small relative to revenue
- Anchored against: office manager ($3,500/mo) + dispatcher ($3,000/mo) + bookkeeper ($2,000/mo) = $8,500/mo
- Represents <1% of revenue for target companies
- **The "freedom" price point — buys back 15-20 hours/week of the owner's time**

### Setup Fee Strategy

Setup fees serve three purposes:
1. **Cash flow** — covers deployment costs and feeds acquisition
2. **Commitment** — clients who pay setup fees churn less (they're invested)
3. **Qualification** — if they can't afford $1,500, they can't afford the monthly either

**Discount strategy:**
- Full price always quoted first
- "Annual prepay" discount: 2 months free if they pay annually ($497 × 10 = $4,970/yr vs $5,964)
- Setup fee can be waived for referrals (lower CAC makes it worthwhile)
- Never discount monthly — only waive/reduce setup
