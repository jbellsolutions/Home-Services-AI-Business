# Home Services AI Business — Full Vertical Strategy

## Executive Summary

We sell AI agent systems to home services companies (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, cleaning, pest control, garage doors, painting, etc.) that solve their #1 problem: **they're great at the trade but terrible at running the business.**

The average home services company loses $10K-$30K/month in revenue from missed calls, unfollowed estimates, zero review management, and no customer lifecycle marketing. We fix all of it with AI agents that run 24/7.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Pick One Trade, Dominate It (Month 1-3)

**Start with HVAC.** Why:
- Highest average job value ($350 residential, $5K-$15K commercial installs)
- Strong seasonal patterns (easy seasonal campaign angles)
- Maintenance agreements = recurring revenue = lifetime value story
- They already spend heavily on marketing ($5K-$15K/mo on ads)
- Most pain from missed calls (emergency calls at night/weekends)
- Easiest ROI story: "You missed 45 calls last month. At $500 average job, that's $22,500 in lost revenue."

**Target:** HVAC companies doing $1M-$5M revenue, 5-25 employees, spending on Google Ads/LSA, active Google Business Profile with 50+ reviews.

### Phase 2: Expand to Adjacent Trades (Month 4-6)

Once HVAC is proven with 5-10 clients and case studies:
- **Plumbing** — Same pain points, similar job values, often paired with HVAC
- **Electrical** — Similar structure, emergency services = same missed call problem
- **Roofing** — Highest job values ($5K-$25K), massive estimate follow-up problem

### Phase 3: Full Home Services Portfolio (Month 7-12)

- Landscaping, cleaning, pest control, garage doors, painting, flooring
- Multi-trade operators (companies that do HVAC + plumbing + electrical)
- Franchise locations (5-50 locations per franchise)

### Phase 4: Productize and Scale (Month 12+)

- White-label the system for marketing agencies serving home services
- Franchise deployment packages (deploy to 50 locations at once)
- Industry partnerships (ServiceTitan integration, Housecall Pro integration)
